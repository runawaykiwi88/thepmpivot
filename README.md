# The PM Pivot

**An Oregon Trail for the AI age.**

You're a Senior Product Manager at PIVOT.AI. The CEO has announced the company is going all-in on AI — hard. You have 6 sprints, a $200k budget, and a team of 5 developers to ship the new AI-powered onboarding experience.

Balance is everything. Go too AI-first and you'll automate yourself out of a job. Resist too hard and you'll become a technological dinosaur. Somewhere in the middle is survival.

Also: there are office penguins. Try to save them.

## How to Play

Each sprint, allocate 20 story points across four categories:

| Category | Effect |
|---|---|
| 🤖 AI Tools | Fast progress, raises AI Score |
| 👨‍💻 Traditional code | Reliable progress, raises Dino Score |
| 🔧 Tech Debt | Prevents disasters |
| 🐛 Debug AI | Lowers AI Score slightly |

**Fail conditions**
- AI Score ≥ 10 → You're automated out
- Dino Score ≥ 10 → You're a technological dinosaur
- Budget ≤ $0 → Bankrupt
- Morale ≤ 0 → Mass resignation

**Win condition:** Ship the onboarding to 100% by the end of Sprint 6.

There are 7 possible endings. One of them involves penguins.
