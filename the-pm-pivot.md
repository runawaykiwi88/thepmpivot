# THE PM PIVOT
## Survive the AI Revolution (or Die Trying)

### 🎯 THE HOOK

**You're a Product Manager at TechCorp Inc. Your mission: Ship a new onboarding experience in 6 sprints.**

**The twist?** Your company is pivoting to AI. Hard. Every sprint, you must balance:
- 🤖 Using AI tools (fast but risky)
- 👨‍💻 Traditional coding (slow but reliable)
- 🔧 Fixing tech debt (boring but necessary)
- 🐛 Debugging AI hallucinations (the new reality)

**Two ways to lose:**
1. **Go too AI-heavy** → You automate yourself out of a job. Your boss replaces you with a ChatGPT prompt.
2. **Go too traditional** → You're a dinosaur. Fired for "not embracing innovation."

**The only way to survive:** Find the impossible balance.

---

## 📊 THE STATS

### 1. 📦 ONBOARDING PROGRESS
- **Start**: 0%
- **Goal**: 100% = You shipped! (Maybe you survive?)
- **How to increase**: Allocate story points each sprint

### 2. 🤖 AI ADOPTION SCORE
- **Start**: 0/10 (You're a luddite)
- **Danger Zone**: Hit 10/10 = "You're redundant" (LOSE)
- **Increases**: Using AI tools, AI designers, AI features
- **Critical**: At 8+, your boss starts asking "Why do we need you?"

### 3. 🦕 DINOSAUR SCORE  
- **Start**: 0/10 (You're bleeding edge)
- **Danger Zone**: Hit 10/10 = "You're outdated" (LOSE)
- **Increases**: Traditional coding, avoiding AI, manual work
- **Critical**: At 8+, younger PMs start calling you "old school"

### 4. 💰 BUDGET
- **Start**: $200k
- **Burn**: Varies by choices (hiring designers, cloud costs, etc.)
- **Game Over**: Hit $0 = Project cancelled

### 5. 😰 TEAM MORALE
- **Start**: 7/10
- **Effects**: Low morale = slower progress, higher chance of devs quitting
- **Affected by**: Events, choices, how you treat the team

### 6. 🐧 PENGUIN LIVES
- **Start**: 5 penguins
- **Special Rule**: Each time you use AI, 10% chance a random penguin dies
- **Why?**: The AI training data centers are killing Antarctic penguins (it's satire)
- **Effect**: Cosmetic but guilt-inducing. Easter egg ending if you save all 5.

## 📅 THE 6 SPRINTS

### SPRINT 1: "The Kickoff"

**Baseline Situation**: 
"Project kickoff. You need a designer for the onboarding flow mockups."

**Decision 1: The Designer**
- **Option A**: Hire a real designer ($30k)
  - Effect: Quality +high, Dino +1, Budget -$30k
- **Option B**: Use Midjourney + Figma AI ($0)
  - Effect: Quality +medium, AI +2, 20% chance designs are unusable

**Decision 2: The Tech Stack**  
- **Option A**: Use React (tried and true)
  - Effect: Dino +1, slower progress
- **Option B**: Use latest AI-powered framework "VaporJS"
  - Effect: AI +1, 30% chance it breaks mid-project

---

### SPRINT 2: "The First Demo"

**Baseline Situation**:
"You're demoing to stakeholders. The onboarding is 20% done but rough."

**Decision 1: The Polish**
- **Option A**: Manually polish every detail
  - Effect: Dino +1, -5 story points next sprint (time wasted)
- **Option B**: Use AI to generate placeholder copy/images
  - Effect: AI +2, 25% chance of offensive AI-generated content

**Decision 2: The Feature Request**
- Stakeholder: "Can the AI predict what users want before they know?"
- **Option A**: "Sure!" (promise AI feature you can't deliver)
  - Effect: Morale -2 (team knows it's BS), AI +1
- **Option B**: "That's not feasible"
  - Effect: Stakeholder -annoyed, but honest

---

### SPRINT 3: "The Crisis Sprint"

**Baseline Situation**:
"Three devs are out sick/PTO at the same time. You're understaffed."

**Decision 1: The Replacement**
- **Option A**: Hire expensive contractors ($50k)
  - Effect: Budget -$50k, progress continues
- **Option B**: Use GitHub Copilot to write their code
  - Effect: AI +3, 40% chance code is broken

**Decision 2: The Scope Creep**
- Boss: "Can we add a chatbot to the onboarding?"
- **Option A**: "Absolutely!" (add more work)
  - Effect: Morale -1, AI +2, need +10 story points
- **Option B**: "Not in scope" (push back)
  - Effect: Boss -annoyed, but realistic

**RANDOM EVENT** (guaranteed this sprint):
"Your security badge stops working. The office switched to AI facial recognition. You spend 45 minutes arguing with a camera."
- Effect: Lose 3 story points (wasted time)

---

### SPRINT 4: "The Pivot"

**Baseline Situation**:
"Your boss saw a competitor's onboarding. 'Ours needs to be MORE AI.'"

**Decision 1: The Redesign**
- **Option A**: Rebuild with AI-first approach
  - Effect: AI +3, lose 20% progress, start over partially
- **Option B**: Add superficial "AI" badges to existing design
  - Effect: AI +1, no real change, boss happy

**Decision 2: The All-Hands**
- Announcement: "TechCorp is restructuring. Some roles are redundant."
- **Option A**: Panic and go all-in on AI to prove value
  - Effect: AI +2, team morale -2 (they know you're scared)
- **Option B**: Stay the course, trust your plan
  - Effect: No change, but the anxiety is real

**RANDOM EVENT** (50% chance):
"New AI tool 'PromptGenius' launches. Everyone's using it."
- **Option A**: Adopt it immediately
  - Effect: AI +1, 30% chance it leaks your company data
- **Option B**: Stick with current tools
  - Effect: Dino +1, you look out of touch

---

### SPRINT 5: "The Breakdown"

**Baseline Situation**:
"You're at 65% done. The finish line is close. But chaos looms."

**Decision 1: The LinkedIn Post**
- You spend 3 days crafting the perfect AI-optimized LinkedIn post about your journey.
- **Option A**: Post it (waste time for clout)
  - Effect: AI +1, lose 6 story points, morale -1 (team sees you're distracted)
- **Option B**: Don't post it (focus on work)
  - Effect: No change, but you feel uncool

**Decision 2: The Mandatory Team Building**
- HR: "Mandatory archery team building tomorrow."
- **During event**: A developer gets shot with an arrow (minor injury).
- **Option A**: Send them home, continue with reduced team
  - Effect: Morale +1 (you care), lose 4 story points
- **Option B**: "Walk it off, we have a sprint to finish"
  - Effect: Morale -3, full story points

**RANDOM EVENT** (guaranteed):
"One of your devs' personal AI agent accidentally sent a resignation email after he complained about stress on Slack."
- Effect: Lose 1 developer permanently, -8 story points next sprint

---

### SPRINT 6: "The Final Push"

**Baseline Situation**:
"Final sprint. You're at ~75-85% done. This is it."

**Decision 1: The Finish Line**
- **Option A**: Use AI to auto-complete the remaining features
  - Effect: AI +3, 50% chance of critical bugs, instant 100% progress
- **Option B**: Grind it out manually with the team
  - Effect: Dino +2, slower but reliable, guaranteed completion

**Decision 2: The Phishing Training**
- IT: "Mandatory phishing awareness training. 4 hours. Today."
- **Option A**: Attend (lose half a day)
  - Effect: Lose 5 story points
- **Option B**: Skip it (focus on shipping)
  - Effect: 25% chance you fall for actual phishing later (lose all credentials)

   - Effect: Cosmetic (penguin counter -1), but emotionally devastating

   - 6 hours of meetings about the future of AI at the company.
   - Effect: Lose 4 story points, morale -1

4. **"The Prompt Engineer Hire"**
   - Your boss hires a "Prompt Engineer" who earns more than you.
   - Effect: Morale -2, AI +1 (they help), but also you feel obsolete

5. **"The Regulation"**
   - New AI regulation requires 3 weeks of compliance work.
   - Effect: Lose 10 story points OR pay $40k fine

6. **"The AI Writes Your Standup"**
   - You let AI write your standup notes. It says you "accomplished nothing."
   - Effect: Boss questions your value, morale -1

## 🔧 TECHNICAL IMPLEMENTATION

### Game State Object

```javascript
const gameState = {
  sprint: 1,
  onboardingProgress: 0,
  aiScore: 0,
  dinoScore: 0,
  budget: 200000,
  morale: 7,
  penguins: 5,
  techDebt: 0, // accumulates if ignored
  eventLog: [],
  storyPointsPerSprint: 20,
  developerCount: 5, // can decrease from events
};
```

### Story Point Allocation

```javascript
// Each sprint, player allocates 20 points across:
allocation = {
  aiTools: 0,        // +AI score, fast progress, penguin risk
  traditional: 0,    // +Dino score, slow but reliable
  techDebt: 0,       // prevents disasters, no score change
  debugAI: 0,        // fixes AI mistakes, required if AI breaks
};

// Progress calculation:
progressGain = (aiTools * 2.5) + (traditional * 1.5) + (techDebt * 0.5);
aiScore += Math.floor(aiTools / 4);
dinoScore += Math.floor(traditional / 4);

// Penguin check:
if (aiTools > 0) {
  if (Math.random() < 0.1) penguins--;
}
```
